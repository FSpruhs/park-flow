# Customer Access Bounded Context

Specific terms related to customer access.

| Term                         | Definition                                                                                    |
|------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Customer Parking Spot Rental | A reservation connecting a customer to a rentable parking spot.                               |
| Payment Method               | A method a customer uses to pay for parking services, e.g., credit card or paypal.            |
| Customer List                | The collection of all registered customers in the system, including their vehicles.           |
| Parking Spot Catalog         | The inventory of all rentable parking spots, including their types, availability, and status. |
